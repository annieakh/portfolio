About Me
Hey, I’m Annie!
A Web Developer & Designer
A Web Developer and Brand Specialist dedicated to creating impactful digital experiences for businesses and individuals. I combine modern design principles with reliable development practices to build websites that look polished, work seamlessly, and perform effectively across different devices.
Every project starts with understanding what makes your business unique. I take the time to learn about your objectives, target audience, and requirements so I can develop a website that reflects your brand and supports your goals. Rather than focusing only on appearance, I build with usability, functionality, and long-term performance in mind.
My core expertise lies in MERN Stack development, along with extensive experience working with WordPress and Shopify. I work with technologies including JavaScript, React.js, Node.js, Express.js, PHP, HTML, and CSS, giving me the flexibility to handle both front-end interfaces and back-end functionality. From business websites and online stores to custom web applications, I create solutions tailored to each project's specific requirements.
I also pay close attention to the factors that keep a website successful after launch. This includes SEO, security, speed optimization, responsive layouts, technical improvements, and ongoing maintenance. My aim is to deliver digital products that are not only attractive but also fast, dependable, easy to use, and ready to grow with your business.
